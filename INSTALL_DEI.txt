Faction Switch (DeI) for Total War: Rome II - Divide et Impera, Grand Campaign
============================================================================

Play several factions in one DeI campaign. A "Switch faction" button (top left, next to the menu buttons) opens a
list of the playable factions with DeI's names. Pick one and press OK: the game saves, the helper makes a copy of that
save with the chosen faction as the player, and the game loads it (one to two minutes). Your old faction is then
played by the AI.

Requirements
- Divide et Impera (Part 1, 2 and 3). The DeI 1.3.6 Public Beta is optional: the mod works with DeI 1.3.5 alone or
  with the beta.

Install
1. Subscribe on the Steam Workshop, or put @@@@@@PAW_faction_switch_dei.pack into the game's data folder:
     ...\steamapps\common\Total War Rome II\data\
   Enable it in the launcher's mod manager. Put it above Divide et Impera, and above the 1.3.6 beta if you use it.
2. Unzip faction_switch_folder.zip into the game folder, so that the folder faction_switch sits next to Rome2.exe:
     ...\steamapps\common\Total War Rome II\faction_switch\faction_switch_helper.exe
     ...\steamapps\common\Total War Rome II\faction_switch\missions\main_rome_98d3fd13aef9.esf  (DeI 1.3.5)
     ...\steamapps\common\Total War Rome II\faction_switch\missions\main_rome_d50b7e641ddb.esf  (DeI 1.3.6 beta)
     ...\steamapps\common\Total War Rome II\faction_switch\missions\main_rome_08ad6bd45ad7.esf  (vanilla)
   The same folder serves the vanilla and the DeI version.

Use
- Switch only during your own turn. Switching ends your current turn. While the switch runs, a window says "please
  wait"; its X button cancels.
- During the load the helper moves your mouse for a few clicks in the Load window (its OK and the confirm tick).
  Don't touch the mouse for those few seconds.
- Each switch makes a new save named "FS <faction> t<turn> <time>" in your save folder.

Limits
- Grand Campaign (main_rome) only.
- A faction you play for the first time gets its victory conditions and chapter objectives from a copy of the
  campaign start (faction_switch\missions); without that folder it gets none.
- Not compatible with the vanilla Faction Switch pack (use one of them) or with other mods that replace
  campaigns/main_rome/scripting.lua or ui/campaign ui/campaign_tactical_map. Works with Consul Scriptum.
- DeI's rules for the player's faction follow the faction you play; how they act after a switch is not checked in
  detail. Battles fought by hand after a switch are not tested.
- Windows only. The helper is a program (built with PyInstaller); some antivirus programs flag such programs.

Files the pack replaces: campaigns/main_rome/scripting.lua (DeI's file plus two lines that load the mod),
ui/campaign ui/campaign_tactical_map.
Logs (for bug reports): faction_switch\lua.log and faction_switch\helper.log.
