Faction Switch for Total War: Rome II (vanilla Grand Campaign)
==============================================================

Play several factions in one campaign. A "Switch faction" button (top left, next to the menu buttons) opens a list of
the playable factions. Pick one and press OK: the game saves, the helper makes a copy of that save with the chosen
faction as the player, and the game loads it (about a minute). Your old faction is then played by the AI.

Install
1. Put faction_switch.pack into the game's data folder:
     ...\steamapps\common\Total War Rome II\data\
   and enable it in the launcher's mod manager.
2. Put the folder faction_switch (with faction_switch_helper.exe and the missions folder inside) into the game
   folder, next to Rome2.exe:
     ...\steamapps\common\Total War Rome II\faction_switch\faction_switch_helper.exe
     ...\steamapps\common\Total War Rome II\faction_switch\missions\main_rome_08ad6bd45ad7.esf

Use
- Switch only during your own turn. While the switch runs, a window says "please wait"; its X button cancels.
- During the load the helper moves your mouse for a few clicks in the Load window (its OK and the confirm tick).
  Don't touch the mouse for those few seconds.
- Each switch makes a new save named "FS <faction> t<turn> <time>" in your save folder.

Limits
- Vanilla Grand Campaign (main_rome) only.
- A faction you play for the first time gets its victory conditions and chapter objectives from a copy of the
  campaign start (faction_switch\missions); without that folder it gets none.
- Not compatible with mods that replace campaigns/main_rome/scripting.lua (for example Divide et Impera) or
  ui/campaign ui/campaign_tactical_map. Works with Consul Scriptum.
- Windows only. The helper is a program (built with PyInstaller); some antivirus programs flag such programs.

Files the pack replaces: campaigns/main_rome/scripting.lua (vanilla file plus two lines that load the mod),
ui/campaign ui/campaign_tactical_map.
Logs (for bug reports): faction_switch\lua.log and faction_switch\helper.log.
